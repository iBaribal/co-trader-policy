CoTrader – Data Deletion Instructions

CoTrader, operated by Bytengine OÜ (Kangru tee 19/2-4, Lubja küla, Viimsi vald, 74010 Harju maakond, Estonia), stores only public Threads posts that mention a listed company or cryptocurrency (see the privacy policy). No one outside Bytengine OÜ has an account.

  1. Email fred.martmaa@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Threads data”.
  2. Include your Threads username, and the post links if you want only specific posts removed.
  3. All stored posts by that username (or the listed posts) are deleted within 30 days, and you receive a confirmation by email.

If you removed the CoTrader app's access in your Threads settings, nothing further is collected through your account; public posts already stored are deleted on request as above, and in any case after 12 months.
