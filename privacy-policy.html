CoTrader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

CoTrader is an internal research application operated by Bytengine OÜ, Kangru tee 19/2-4, Lubja küla, Viimsi vald, 74010 Harju maakond, Estonia (“we”). It studies whether public discussion of companies and cryptocurrencies relates to later market prices. It has no users outside Bytengine OÜ, offers no accounts to the public and shows no ads.

What we collect from Threads

  • Public posts returned by keyword or topic searches through the official Threads API (keyword search): the post ID, text, permalink, timestamp, author username and whether the post is a reply.
  • The Threads account of Bytengine OÜ's operator: the access token that authorizes the app and the account ID. No other Threads user signs in to CoTrader.

We do not collect private messages, follower lists, profile details beyond the username shown with a public post, email addresses, contacts, location or device information.

How we use it

  • Posts are scanned automatically for mentions of listed companies and cryptocurrencies. Posts that mention none are discarded immediately and never stored.
  • Posts that do mention one are stored and counted as part of aggregate attention statistics (how many distinct stories and sources discuss an asset), which are compared with later price movements.
  • Individual posts are visible only to Bytengine OÜ in a private dashboard running on its own computer.

We do not sell, rent, share, publish or transfer Threads data to anyone, do not use it for advertising, and do not build profiles of individual people.

Storage, security and retention

Data is stored in a database on Bytengine OÜ's own computer, which is not reachable from the internet. Stored posts are kept for research for up to 12 months and then deleted. The access token is stored in a file readable only by the operator's computer account.

Deletion

To have a post or all posts of an account deleted from CoTrader, follow the data deletion instructions. Posts deleted on Threads are not refreshed by CoTrader and are removed with the 12-month retention at the latest, or immediately on request.
